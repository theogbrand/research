Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is one of only three surviving sovereign city-states in the world (along with Monaco and Vatican City), but here's the surprising part — it is actually made up of 64 islands, not just one. Most people picture Singapore as a single, compact island, but it includes dozens of smaller islands like Sentosa, Pulau Ubin, and St. John's Island. Even more surprising, Singapore's total land area has grown by over 25% since independence in 1965 through massive land reclamation projects, literally building new land from the sea. The country has expanded from about 581 square kilometers to over 733 square kilometers, and plans to add even more by the 2030s. So this tiny city-state is actually getting bigger — one truckload of sand at a time.
