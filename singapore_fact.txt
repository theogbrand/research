Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is one of only three surviving sovereign city-states in the world (along with Monaco and Vatican City), but here's the surprising part: it is actually made up of 63 islands, not just one. Most people picture Singapore as a single island, but beyond the main island there are dozens of smaller ones, including Pulau Ubin (a rustic island with wild boars and abandoned quarries), Sentosa, and St. John's Island. Some of these islands are so small and obscure that even many Singaporeans have never visited — or even heard of — them. The country has also been steadily growing its land area through massive reclamation projects, increasing its size by over 25% since independence in 1965, essentially building new ground from scratch in the sea.
