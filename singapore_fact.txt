Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is one of only three surviving sovereign city-states in the world — but here's what most people don't know: it actually consists of not just one island, but 63 islands. Beyond the famous main island, Singapore includes tiny islands like Pulau Ubin (a rustic throwback with wild boars and abandoned quarries), St. John's Island (once a quarantine station for cholera), and Pedra Branca (a remote rocky outcrop so small it only fits a lighthouse). Even more surprising, Singapore's total number of islands keeps changing — because the country has been aggressively reclaiming land from the sea since the 1960s, growing its total land area by over 25% (from 581 km² to roughly 733 km²). Some of its "islands" today didn't even exist a few decades ago. Singapore is literally building itself, one truckload of sand at a time.
