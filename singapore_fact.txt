Singapore has vending machines that dispense luxury cars! Located in a 15-story building called Autobahn Motors, this automated car showroom uses a giant vending machine system where buyers can select their dream car (including Bentleys, Ferraris, and Lamborghinis) from a touchscreen, and watch as a robotic system retrieves and delivers their vehicle within minutes. It was created to solve Singapore's limited land space problem - instead of sprawling car lots, the entire inventory is stored vertically like a mechanical parking garage, making it the world's largest luxury car vending machine.
