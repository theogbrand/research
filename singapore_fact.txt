Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is home to a water reservoir that sits on top of a dam built inside the city's Marina Bay — making it one of the few countries in the world with a freshwater reservoir in the heart of its downtown. The Marina Barrage, completed in 2008, turned the entire Marina Basin into a freshwater lake by damming the mouth of the bay. This means that right beneath the glittering skyline of Marina Bay Sands and the financial district, there's a massive freshwater supply serving the nation. Singapore essentially turned its iconic city center waterfront from saltwater to freshwater, combining urban planning, flood control, and water security into a single engineering marvel — a feat no other major city has replicated at this scale.
