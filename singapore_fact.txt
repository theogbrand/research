Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is one of only three surviving sovereign city-states in the world (alongside Monaco and Vatican City), but here's the truly surprising part — it is actually made up of 64 islands, not just one. Most people picture Singapore as a single, compact island, but it includes dozens of smaller islands like Sentosa, Pulau Ubin, and St. John's Island. Even more remarkable, Singapore's total land area has grown by over 25% since independence in 1965 through extensive land reclamation from the sea. The country literally builds itself bigger, importing sand and filling in coastal areas to create new land — transforming from 581.5 square kilometers to over 730 square kilometers today. Singapore has been quietly growing its own territory for decades, one truckload of sand at a time.
