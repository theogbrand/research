Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is home to a building that contains an entire forest — the Jewel Changi Airport features a 130-foot (40-meter) indoor waterfall called the Rain Vortex, the tallest indoor waterfall in the world, surrounded by a terraced garden with over 2,000 trees and 100,000 shrubs. But here's the part most people don't know: the waterfall is actually part of the building's air conditioning system. The cascading water cools the surrounding air naturally, reducing the energy needed to climate-control the massive complex. So what looks like a breathtaking piece of architecture is secretly a giant, gorgeous cooling mechanism disguised as a tourist attraction.
