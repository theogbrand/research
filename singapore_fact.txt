Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is home to a water supply system so advanced that it recycles used water into ultra-clean, high-grade drinking water called "NEWater." The purification process — involving microfiltration, reverse osmosis, and UV disinfection — produces water that is actually cleaner than regular tap water. NEWater now meets up to 40% of Singapore's total water demand, and the country plans to increase that to 55% by 2060. What makes this truly surprising is that Singapore essentially turns sewage into some of the purest drinking water on Earth, and it's been doing so since 2003 — long before most countries even considered large-scale water recycling.
