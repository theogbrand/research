Fun Fact About Singapore:

Singapore is home to the world's first "Night Safari" — a wildlife park that operates entirely after dark. Opened in 1994, it spans 35 hectares of secondary rainforest and allows visitors to observe over 900 nocturnal animals from 100 species in naturalistic habitats illuminated by subtle moonlight-simulating lighting. What makes this especially surprising is that the lighting technology was custom-engineered to be bright enough for human visitors to see the animals, yet dim enough that the animals behave as if they are still in complete darkness, going about their natural nighttime routines undisturbed. No other zoo in the world had attempted anything like this before Singapore pioneered the concept.
